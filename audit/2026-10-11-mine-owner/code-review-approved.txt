`run` validates width, grid limits, alignment, and mapping before it can set `failed`. Count 0 copies `[0; 32]` and does not submit. The grid is `count/k + (count%k != 0)` for k = 1, 2, or 4, with checked conversion and no padded lanes. Parameters are an explicit 56-byte little-endian image with a zero pad. `failed = true`, `submit`, and `wait` are adjacent.
